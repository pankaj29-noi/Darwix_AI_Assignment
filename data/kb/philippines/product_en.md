# Product

This bancassurance plan is a life insurance policy introduced through a partner bank. The bank referral does not turn the policy into a deposit. Premium can be debited from the savings account. Choose a beneficiary and an optional accident rider. If the debit fails before the due date, the policy can lapse. This is synthetic demo data.
