# Coverage

Pre-existing conditions are covered after a waiting period of 36 months. Room rent on the base plan is capped at 1 percent of the sum insured per day. Claims should be reported within 30 days of discharge.

# Exclusions

The policy does not pay cosmetic surgery. Pre-existing condition declarations must be completed on the proposal form.
