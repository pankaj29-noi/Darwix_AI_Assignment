# Qualification

A lead is qualified when age is from 18 to 65 inclusive, the city is serviceable, and the stated budget is at least 500 rupees per month. Serviceable cities are Pune, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Jaipur, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, and Lucknow. Customers outside this age band are not eligible for Darwix Health Shield. Qualification is preliminary and is not a final underwriting decision.
